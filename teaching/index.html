  1. In my graduate work, I developed a 3D noise variance estimation algorithm from scratch. This later served as a part of an image denoising algorithm. (I implemented it in python)

  2. I also trained a UNet model for image segmentation(semantic) and partial convolutions inpainting with tensorflow. (Retrained with pytorch)

  3. I developed a hybrid-ML inpainting algorithm for reconstructing electron microscopy images. The same can be applied to other types of data by modifying the loss functions. (Originally with matlab, but moved to python)

  4. I built a python library called pyEBSD for processing microstructure data of material: the library measures crystal misorientation and disorientations, crystal symmetries, visualize orientation maps using ipf-coloration, denoise and inpaint orientation maps. (It is a python library)

  5. When training my UNet model, I performed my hyper-parameter tuning experiments with an experimental design using a full factorial design to do a total sweep of the search space, and to identify which parameters were significant.

  6. In my time at Regeneron, I created an experimental design for a toxicology study to test dose-dependent toxicity of a drug via intravenous injection. The proposed study was in two part. The experimental design, and the statistical comparisons of the different factors that were measures and their corresponding levels. I performed what I called “Statistical Residual/Distillation Analysis” to model controlled features, while accounting for potentially confounding factors using linear regression.

  7. I also developed a sampling plan to determine whether supplies in a warehouse were exposed to contamination, and I prepared a full statistical report in accordance with GxP stating the probabilistic reasoning and justification behind the choices made.

  8. At Clarkson University, I trained a high-risk student detection model and used hypothesis testing to quantify the effectiveness of retention interventions. The model and paper are publicly available.

  9. I also built a weather-based customer demand forecasting model for a local restaurant which helped reduce their food waste by up to 30% within a 3 month period. I scrapped historical weather forecast data into my local sql (sqlite) database and trained various models including; linear regression, gradient boosting, random forest, and a neural network with a single hidden layer.

  10. I built an image processing application using flask for simple everyday tasks; scaling, feature detection, background removal(inpainting), and image morphing.

  11. I just started a position at the univesity of Toledo as an assistant professor, where I teach Mathematics and Data Science courses.
